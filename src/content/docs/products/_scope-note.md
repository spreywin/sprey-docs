---
title: Processing Scope Note
description: Concise statement of the Sprey Processing product boundary.
---

`pay.sprey.win` is the Sprey Processing reference deployment.

Sprey Processing covers the broader BTCPay merchant payment model: online-store integrations, in-person Point of Sale, QR payment flows, Payment Requests, Payment Buttons and donations, crowdfunding, and API/custom integrations.

`Sprey WP Stack` is one ready-made WordPress/WooCommerce implementation of the online-commerce path. It is not the boundary of Sprey Processing.

Merchant payments go to merchant-controlled wallets or payment destinations. Sprey does not initiate, route, receive, hold, or forward merchant funds.
