The Products section documents live Sprey products, intended capabilities, and verification checkpoints. [Sprey Lab](/products/sprey-lab/) is the separate experimental area for BTCPay applications and plugin-based flows; Lab test results are recorded separately from commercial Processing verification.

The central product is **Sprey Processing** at `pay.sprey.win`: **your own non-custodial payment infrastructure — hosted and managed by Sprey.** It is broader than any single storefront stack and supports online commerce, in-person acceptance, direct payment requests, donations and buttons, crowdfunding, and API/custom integration paths as those capabilities are configured and verified.

Merchant payments go directly to merchant-controlled wallets or payment destinations. Sprey does not receive, hold, exchange, transfer, or otherwise control merchant funds.
